# Session 01 – Quantum ML Cohort Introduction

## Main goal

The cohort focuses on quantum machine learning, but the sessions first build a foundation in quantum computing from a computer-science perspective.

## Quantum computing and quantum advantage

The session introduced the idea of quantum advantage: researchers investigate whether a quantum computer can process certain tasks faster than a classical computer.

The instructor emphasized that the cohort will first focus on the fundamentals needed to understand quantum computing rather than jumping directly into quantum machine learning.

## Four data/computer combinations

The session introduced four combinations:

- **CC:** classical data + classical computer
- **CQ:** classical data + quantum computer
- **QC:** quantum data + classical computer
- **QQ:** quantum data + quantum computer

For QC, quantum information has to be transformed into classical information before it can be processed by a classical computer.

## Book roadmap

The textbook has seven chapters:

1. Classical information and classical computation
2. One qubit
3. Linear algebra
4. Multiple qubits
5. Quantum programming with Qiskit
6. Entanglement and quantum protocols
7. Quantum algorithms / next steps

## Chapters 1–4

The first four chapters are individual learning. Participants can keep their notes and code in a personal GitHub repository and share notebooks or questions in the cohort Discord.

## Open-source project

The cohort will later work collaboratively on an open-source quantum visualizer using Qiskit. The project is intended to help beginners understand quantum concepts through explanations, examples, code, and visualizations.

## Preparation

The instructor recommended refreshing the fundamentals of linear algebra before Chapter 1, including concepts such as linear combinations, linear independence/dependence, determinants, conjugates, transpose, and standard bases.
